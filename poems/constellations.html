The Weight of Time — Recull 1

Constellations

Constel·lacions

Five poems on the making, holding, and loss of pattern in an indifferent sky.

The Firmament

Night opens over the dry earth.
The air stands still between the mountains.
The ground gives back no light.
Everything rests inside the dark.
Night animals call out.
Points of light appear slowly.
One point. Another. Thousands.
Scattered stars cross the black.
Separated by distances the eye cannot grasp.
Distance and emptiness fill the firmament.
No star needs the others.
No path between them.
No line.
The gaze travels the sky.
The stars are.
Only scattered points over the night.
The dark demands no form.
The dark is.

The Line

The gaze searches.
One point draws near another.
The gesture is minimal.
The emptiness immense.
A line crosses the eye.
Then another.
The points hold angles.
The distances become segments.
The mind wants relation.
It hates the silence of the void.
The figure takes shape.
Structure emerging from emptiness.
The stars remain motionless.
None of them knows the line that joins them.
The relation exists in the gaze.

The Name

A word for the outline that did not exist before.
The name echoes through the night.
The stars are now a body.
An animal.
A dream.
A ship.
Other points become a trail.
The movement of a hunt across the horizon.
The figure rises into the sky and becomes stable.
The name passes between voices.
Maps.
Tales, myths, and histories.
The sky is mute, as always.
The stars remain indifferent.
But the drawing is real.

The Movement

Over the years the sky shifts slowly.
The positions change, imperceptible.
An angle stops fitting.
A star abandons the line.
The figure deforms.
The names repeat.
The maps are kept.
The stories persist.
The figure is.
By inertia.
Memory holds shapes the sky no longer keeps.
The night
turns without the human lines.

The Stars

In time the lines are erased.
The names fall silent.
The firmament returns to scattered points in the night.
The stories are lost.
The line no longer exists.
The night is night again.
Only the light of the stars remains, in silence.
The light arrives from a past no one watches.
The sky keeps no scars.
Perhaps new lines will come.
New gazes.
New stories.
New truths.
Everything is real.
Nothing has existed.
Everything matters.
Nothing is real.
The stars are.
