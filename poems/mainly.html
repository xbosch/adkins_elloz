11337 — Recull 6

Mainly

Two poems on unmarked labor, and on the disguises a self wears to be seen.

People

People like me
work barren ground,
dry,
digging without rest,
the earth does not yield,
the sun passes
without leaving a mark.

People like me
pass in a dense
silence,
no trace
or memory.

We understand
and do not understand,
the word rasps,
it is not enough to stop it.

Like a clock
always half a second late,
the light is there,
it does not see us.

The hands,
tensed forearms,
on the ground,
draw back,
and we keep taking our rest,
sitting at the edge,
knowing it will come back,
unable to stop it.

And forgiveness,
if there ever was any,
no longer flows.

Participant

The animal that knows it is
hides behind
necessary complexity
finds relation
buries
the need
disguises itself
as beauty
they speak
of what they are not
on waking
the skin falls
the waves
come in
the door
stays half-open
