The Fragility of Basalt

Three poems: a burning world, a beauty that withers as it arrives, a self worn down to a feather.

Murmurs

The world burns.
I have lost the names of things.
The air thickens.
It cannot be breathed.
The sea boils.
The souls depart.
The world will be born from the ash.
But now
the fire
takes everything.

The slopes burn.
The rivers spit lava.
The world comes undone.
The names do not return.
Only the murmur
remains.
I do not remember
my own name.

Anticipation

The landscape
where beauty
already withers as it's born.

Anticipation
crowns the waiting.
The object
is missing.
Empty faces.

Passing glances.
The wonder
is not there.

Anticipation,
ridge
of the void.

Attainment,
the exhumation
of being.

What remains
is the waiting
collapsed
into salt.

Conclusion

I exist
in the time I have no more of,
in every imperfect future.
The hand that hesitates.
The shadow and the light.
I am a mirage
in time.
Hidden
in these lines,
behind
the piercing verses.
I was sea basalt,
impregnable,
become
a bird's feather
that falls
as it lifts into flight.
Without trace.
Without weight.
The time it takes
to disappear.

Time gives no meaning.
Only time
remains.
I rest
in the hardness of these lines.
