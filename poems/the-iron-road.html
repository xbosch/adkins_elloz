The Weight of Time — Recull 3

The Iron Road

Via de Ferro

Eight poems following a valley, a ridge, and a locomotive that wears itself down by moving.

The Valley

Closed valley, no river running down.
Fog soaks into everything.
Static drops on the rock.
Walls sweating cold.
Fog gathered between the slopes.
Visibility to ten paces.
Faint light entering from above.
Dissolved outlines, formless shapes.
Air dense, stagnant.
Damp clinging to the stone.
Silence trapped between walls.
Invisible depths.
Space sunken and saturated.
Only the peaks break the surface.

Ridge

Ridge in the wind.
Constant wind on the crest.
Visibility to the horizon.
Rock dry, bare.
Direct light on the relief.
Hard shadows under the outcrops.
The sun dries out the damp.
The horizon clean and clear, the sea beyond.
Slopes open to the void.
Textures visible, unveiled.
Air crossing the ridge.
Particles scattered by the wind.

Locomotive

Locomotive crossing the landscape.
It stops at no station.
Thrust along the iron road.
Connecting rods in constant tension.
Wheels off-axis under load.
Uneven gears.
Teeth scraping on contact.
Boiler running at sustained pressure.
Regular whistles down the pipe.
No one knows where the steam engine is.
Or what feeds it.
Friction at every turn.
Pistons out of phase.
Continuous structural vibration.
Axles that never quite align.
Continuous movement.
The machine advances without stopping.

The Station

Straight rails, no switches.
In the distance, the station.
Direct approach.
Fixed platform under the roof.
Motionless white light.
Signs aligned and still.
Empty benches on the platform.
Cold concrete beneath the rails.
A steady signal to one side.
The locomotive enters without slowing.
Metallic noise under the roof.
Smoke scattered in the closed air.
It does not stop.
The rails continue beyond.
Silence fills the carriage like a volume of air.
The vibration persists in the structure.

The Passing

Whistling in the distance.
Ridge open to the wind.
A firm trunk at the edge.
Leaves under direct light.
The vibration runs through the ground.
The train approaches quickly.
Low smoke among the branches.
A brief shadow on the bark.
Wheels on a constant grade.
Sustained pressure.
The valley falls behind.
The train passes.
The sharp whistle recedes.
The mechanical noise fades.

Torsion

Opposing forces on the same axis.
Tension at the core.
Latent deformation within.
The load holds.
The material remains unknown.
Sustained pressure under conflict.
The structure twists slowly.
There is no glory in the turning.
Nothing is released.
The system stays under tension.
It advances under contradiction.
It twists.
It does not break.
The train advances.

Inertia

Load sustained along its course.
Initial thrust spent.
No visible impulse.
Speed held by mass alone.
Every station left behind.
The rails fix the direction.
No clear origin.
No functional destination.
The system does not stop.
It advances by inertia, meter by meter.
Movement sustained with no engine running.
The mass stays on course.

Entropy

Metal dust over the track.
Residue of constant friction.
Gear teeth worn blunt.
Steam leaking at the joints.
Loss of material at every station.
Heat dissipating through the structure.
The machine groans.
Growing noise in the mechanism.
A trail of iron left behind.
Accumulated wear.
The locomotive consumes itself under load.
Movement kept up by its own wearing away.
Less useful mass.
More vibration.
The matter returns to the track.
The advance costs what the machine is.
