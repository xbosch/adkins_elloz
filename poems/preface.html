The Weight of Time — Preface

Preface

To read these poems is to stop searching for a meaning behind things, and to accept their presence instead. The world is. Meaning, when it appears, is local and provisional. There is no final structure, no story, no foundation holding up what exists. This does not open onto emptiness. What is needs nothing.

On this same plane, the self does not govern or decide. It yields. It is a body acted upon, crossed by passions, subject to movement. It is not resolved or explained. It appears, holds, transforms.

There is no conclusion. The movement continues. This position offers no refuge, but it does not need one either. It relaxes the need to understand, and leaves what is within what is.

These lines do not hold themselves up.

Translated from the Catalan, “Prefaci.”
