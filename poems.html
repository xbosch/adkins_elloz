A Book of Poems

The Weight of Time

El Pes del Temps

Xavier B. Lluís · La Crescenta, California · April 2026

Translated here into English for the first time. Six collections move from a sky without paths, through a naked and orderless world, along an iron road that wears itself down by moving, out to open water, into a landscape on fire, and back to unmarked, ordinary labor — closing with the poems that give the book its name.
