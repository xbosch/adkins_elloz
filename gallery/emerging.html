Earliest Work

Emerging

The earliest surviving work, 2021 to 2023 — before Horizons, before Symbols. Four pieces already speak in flat color and a settled gesture. The other four, all from a single afternoon in July 2022, move looser and more atmospheric: a hand still finding the restraint that the later series would hold to.
